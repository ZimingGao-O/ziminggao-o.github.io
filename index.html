Dissertation research · HKUXR interaction

Small gestures,
occupied hands.

How can people issue simple XR commands while both hands are holding or working with an object?

At Situated Reality Lab, I am exploring small movements that remain possible within a fixed grasp. The aim is to issue commands such as “next instruction” or “repeat” while preserving the task in hand.

Current research plan

  • Elicit gestures from users within a concrete task and grasp.
  • Distinguish command gestures from ordinary object handling.
  • Evaluate command input alongside object stability and material state.

Early-stage research · Scenario selection in progress
Target submission: UIST 2027

Situation-Bound Intent

How can proactive AI understand not only what a person wants to remember, but the precise physical situation in which a reminder becomes useful?

SituMemo connects reminder intentions with a shared first-person view. Human-AI grounding helps establish what a situation means to the user before a reminder becomes active.

My contributions

  • Modular wearable hardware design for the application space
  • Visualization of user-study results
  • Refinement and visual consistency of other manuscript figures
